# training change, ledger 8o). Aimed at the disc's dominant loss: 24.5% of ground-truth
# rows sit in the pool at 0.05 < IoU <= 0.5, a population an image-level Dice never
# addresses because one large filament dominates hundreds of marginal ones.
# Weight 0.5 keeps Dice+BCE+clDice as the base; the term is added, not substituted.
